Questions, answered.

Do these QR codes expire?

No. Static codes embed their content directly and have no built-in expiration. If the destination website changes or goes offline, that destination may stop working.

Can I change the destination after printing?

Not with a static QR code. To change a destination later, you'd need to generate a new code. Editable destinations and analytics may be available in a future dynamic QR service.

Which download should I use for printing?

SVG is ideal for print because it scales without losing sharpness. PNG works well for websites, signs and documents. Keep a clear margin around the code and test it with a phone before printing.

Does Vividex store my QR code content?

No. This tool runs in your browser and does not send the entered content to a generation server. Downloads are created on your device.
